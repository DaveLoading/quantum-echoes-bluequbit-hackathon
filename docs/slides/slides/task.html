Quantum Echoes · David

About 40 seconds. Each problem is a quantum echo. Shuffle the qubits with a scrambling circuit U, kick one “butterfly” qubit, then run the shuffle backward. The second-order ones do it twice, with a z flip on the probe qubit in between. For every qubit we report ⟨Z⟩: think of it as how hard that qubit’s coin leans to heads. Scoring is corr² against the exact answer, rounded down to the nearest tenth, times the points, so only the pattern matters. The catch: the big graded circuits have 36 to 38 qubits and up to 7,296 two-qubit gates, past what the free tier can simulate exactly.
