About 40 seconds. G2 is a 36-qubit chain. MPS, a compressed copy of the state, handles most of it, but the free tier caps its quality knob, chi, at 24. Pauli-path alone scored zero: it pruned a whole block of qubits whose true values are near 0.9. So we used each method where it held steady across settings: MPS on 32 qubits and Pauli-path on qubits 22 to 25. 300 became 540. The last tenth needs exact values, which is the limit on the next slide.
